Ticket FAQs

Common Questions

Quick answers about ticket collection, weather policies, and boarding procedures.

Do I need to print my tickets?

No! Digital mobile tickets on your smartphone are fully accepted at turnstiles. Just open the confirmation QR pass on your phone upon arrival.

What if it rains on the day of my visit?

Our passenger coaches feature covered roofs and clear rain guards. If severe gales halt service, we will happily reschedule your tickets free of charge.

Can I change my ticket date after booking?

Yes! Date changes are 100% free up to 24 hours prior to your scheduled arrival. Simply call our visitor desk or reply to your confirmation email.

Can I buy tickets on the day at the station?

Yes, tickets can be purchased at the Central Station Ticket Kiosk on the day. However, booking online guarantees priority carriage boarding during peak periods.
