Good to Know

Party FAQs

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 4 weeks in advance, especially for weekend slots which fill up quickly during school holidays.
Can we bring our own food?
Yes, for the Explorer and Station Master packages. The Royal Express includes full catering, but external food can be arranged with prior notice.
Are adults included in the pricing?
Adult tickets are not included in party packages. Adults accompanying the party pay standard entry fees at the gate.
What if the weather is bad?
All party areas are covered. The train also operates in light rain. We have indoor fallback options for all package types.
