Why Choose Us

A Day of Learning & Discovery

Our school programme combines hands-on railway history, STEM concepts, and outdoor exploration in a safe, welcoming environment.

Our educational sessions are aligned with the National Curriculum, covering history, science, engineering, and environmental studies.

Dedicated education staff lead all sessions. Our DBS-checked guides are trained in child engagement and curriculum delivery.

Dedicated lunch areas for school groups. Pre-ordering available from our café, or bring packed lunches.
