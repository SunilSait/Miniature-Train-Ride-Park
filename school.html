Why Choose Us

A Day of Learning & Discovery

Our school programme combines hands-on railway history, STEM concepts, and outdoor exploration in a safe, welcoming environment.

Curriculum Linked

Our educational sessions are aligned with the National Curriculum, covering history, science, engineering, and environmental studies.

Expert Guides

Dedicated education staff lead all sessions. Our DBS-checked guides are trained in child engagement and curriculum delivery.

Flexible Scheduling

Half-day and full-day options. We work around your timetable, including early arrival for long-distance schools.

Group Discounts

Schools receive significantly reduced rates. Groups of 15+ receive 35% off standard admission. Free spaces for teachers.

Risk Assessed

Full site risk assessments and teacher resource packs provided in advance. Pre-visit support from our education coordinator.

Lunch Facilities

Dedicated lunch areas for school groups. Pre-ordering available from our café, or bring packed lunches and snacks with them.
