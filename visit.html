Onsite

Park Facilities

Everything you need for a comfortable, enjoyable day out in one place.

Café & Refreshments

Hot drinks, light meals, and children's snacks available throughout the day. We cater for dietary requirements.

Picnic Areas

Bring your own food and enjoy our scenic picnic areas. Tables, benches, and grassy areas are available throughout the park.

Accessibility

All areas are wheelchair accessible. We offer wide-carriage trains and dedicated accessible parking close to the entrance.

Gift Shop

Take home a piece of the magic with our range of train-themed gifts, souvenirs, books, and children's toys.

Exhibitions

Explore our indoor railway history exhibition, featuring original artefacts, photographs, and interactive displays for all ages.

Party Facilities

Dedicated birthday and group event spaces. Pre-booking required — see our Birthday Parties page for details.
