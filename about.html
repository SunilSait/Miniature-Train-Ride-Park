Our Story

From a Modest Meadow Loop to a Living Railway Sanctuary

What began in the summer of 1987 as a humble backyard passion project has grown into one of Britain’s most beloved working miniature heritage railway parks.

Our story began with founder Arthur Vance, a retired locomotive fitter who spent thirty years servicing express steam engines on the national railway network. Unwilling to see the art of live-steam boilercraft and traditional mechanical trackwork vanish into history books, Arthur gathered five fellow retired craftsmen and laid the first 200 yards of 15-inch gauge steel in Greenfield Meadow.

Real Working Steam
Every steam locomotive in our fleet is coal-fired and maintained to full mainline commercial pressure standards.
Our Railway History

Chronicles of the 15-Inch Rails: 1987 to Today

Trace the major milestones, engine acquisitions, civil engineering breakthroughs, and trust evolutions that made our railway what it is today.

Summer 1987

The First Greenfield Meadow Spike

Founder Arthur Vance and five volunteers drive the golden spike for the original 200-yard meadow loop using salvaged industrial sidings rail. Petrol shunter "Pippin" pulls the very first 35 passengers.

Foundation 15" Gauge Loop Meadow Halt
Spring 1994

Commissioning of "The Royal Scot" Steam Engine

Our first genuine coal-fired 4-6-2 live steam locomotive enters public service. Designed to authentic one-third scale with functional Walschaerts valve gear, it immediately draws railway enthusiasts from across Europe.

Live Steam Pacific 4-6-2 Coal Fired
Autumn 1999

The Seven-Arch River Viaduct Extension

A major civil engineering undertaking: volunteer stonemasons and civil engineers construct our iconic 7-arch stone-faced viaduct across the River Willow, tripling track length to 1.4 miles.

Stone Viaduct Civil Engineering Track Expansion
Summer 2008

Oakwood Tunnel & Semaphore Signal Box

Completion of the 90-yard Oakwood subterranean tunnel and the commission of a fully functional 16-lever mechanical signal cabin, bringing authentic absolute block signalling regulations to the park.

Oakwood Tunnel Lever Frame Block Signalling
Autumn 2015

Formation of the Heritage Railway Trust

The park transitions to an independent non-profit Charitable Trust, securing the permanent safeguarding of its historic rolling stock, machine shop archives, and educational school bursary funds.

Charitable Trust Heritage Status Education Charter
Spring 2021

Eco-Clean Diesel & Accessible Teak Fleet

Introduction of our low-emission biofuel hydraulic locomotive "Greenfield Ranger" and hand-built teak saloon carriages featuring level roll-on wheelchair ramps and integrated secure restraint bays.

Eco-Biofuel Wheelchair Accessible Teak Carriages
Present Day

A Living Cultural Landmark & STEM Hub

Operating 6 locomotives and 14 carriages over 2.4 miles of line, hosting 80+ school STEM programs every year, and preserving authentic steam railway craft for generations to come.

6 Locomotives 2.4 Miles STEM Hub

Our Commitment to Safety

Uncompromising Standards for Every Passenger

Behind the nostalgic whistle and gentle click of the rails lies a modern, rigorous engineering safety framework backed by an unblemished 38-year operational safety record.

Daily Pre-Service Track & Gauge Audits

Every single morning before the first passenger train departs, certified line inspectors walk the full 2.4-mile circuit checking rail gauge tolerances, track fishplates, point switches, and tree canopy clearances.

  • Optical track alignment & sleeper stability check
  • Flange-way switch point gap inspections
  • Daily signed track clearance certificate

Certified Boiler & Pressure Vessel Testing

Our live steam locomotives operate under the stringent requirements of the Pressure Systems Safety Regulations (PSSR). Independent certified boiler examiners conduct annual ultrasonic and hydraulic tests.

  • Annual 1.5x hydraulic pressure testing
  • Independent non-destructive metal thickness ultrasound
  • Tamper-sealed dual pop safety relief valves
