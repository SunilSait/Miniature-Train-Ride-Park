Scenic Waypoints

The Grand Circuit Journey

Follow our 7¼" gauge mainline through 4 landmark stations and scenic vistas across 15 pristine woodland acres.

00 Min Departure
Mile 0.0 • Station Concourse

Central Victorian Platform

All journeys commence at our faithfully preserved 1920s terminal. Hear the authentic brass station bell, watch coal fueling, and board your carriages safely.

08 Min Crossing
Mile 0.8 • Water Crossing

Willow Creek Trestle

Cross our hand-built timber viaduct soaring above reflective lily ponds. Spot wild mallards, native herons, and enjoy cool breezes across the timber rails.

16 Min Passage
Mile 1.6 • Floral Tunnel

Secret Garden Tunnel

Plunge under our 80-metre fragrant wisteria and climbing rose arbor. Experience the famous whistle echo cavern loved by generations of children and adults.

25 Min Return
Mile 2.4 • Woodland Loop

Oakwood Meadow Loop

The grand turnaround winds through ancient oak groves and wildflower meadows where picnic-goers wave before the locomotive heads homeward to Central Station.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about planning your visit, train schedules, tickets, and park amenities.

Tickets can be purchased either online in advance or on arrival at our Victorian Station Box Office. Online bookings guarantee your preferred departure time slot during peak weekends and seasonal holiday events, so we warmly recommend reserving early.

A complete uninterrupted circuit takes approximately 25 to 30 minutes, covering 2.4 miles of scenic track through meadows, timber bridges, and our famous fragrant floral tunnel. All-Day Passes let you ride as many times as you like throughout your visit!

Yes, absolutely. Our station platform features gently sloped ramp access, and we operate dedicated custom passenger carriages equipped with wide wheelchair boarding ramps and restraint bays. Strollers can be safely parked under our sheltered platform bay.

Our trains run rain or shine! In damp weather we deploy our covered canopy carriages so you remain cozy and dry while rolling through mist-draped woodland. In the rare event of severe weather warnings, tickets can be effortlessly rescheduled at zero fee.
